# Character-art briefs — Borrel 35 typetjes

Six anime character illustrations, one per Kompanen-type, to drop into the site's
per-type **character-art slots** (design: modern-anime × Avatar-the-Last-Airbender
blend, painterly graffiti-alley world). Feed each brief to an AI image tool; keep
the **House style** block identical across all six so they read as one cohesive set.

## House style (prefix every generation with this)

> Modern anime illustration blended with an Avatar: The Last Airbender painterly
> feel. Hand-painted cel-shading, thick expressive line art, rich but harmonious
> colours, visible brush texture and subtle film grain. Character stands in a
> sunlit urban **graffiti alley** — painted concrete wall with spray tags, drips
> and foliage — rendered softly out of focus behind the subject. Warm natural
> light, dramatic but friendly. **Full-body, ¾ dynamic pose, subject centered
> with headroom, portrait orientation (4:5).** All six characters are **tall**
> (the Kompanen / giraffe motif — long limbs, a small giraffe-print accent
> somewhere on the outfit). Consistent art style, line weight and lighting across
> the whole set. Clean, uncluttered margin around the figure so it can be cut out
> and placed on the site (prefer a plain or transparent background edge).

Then append **one** of the six type briefs below. Keep the same seed/style
reference between runs for consistency.

## 1 · De Parkborrelprofessional — signature colour: **pop-gold / amber**

The unshakeable organiser. Plans everything, claims the best terrace spot, runs
the borrel like a gala from the sidelines.

- **Character:** calm, poised, in-command; a warm knowing half-smile, sunglasses
  pushed up into the hair. Clipboard/draaiboek under one arm, a spritz in the
  other hand.
- **Outfit:** smart-casual streetwear — a **gold/amber** utility bomber or vest
  over a crisp tee, tote bag with picnic/park kit, clean sneakers.
- **Pose:** confident, one hand gesturing "it's handled", weight relaxed.
- **Setting tint:** sunny park-wall graffiti in gold/amber, leafy plants, a
  **BORREL** tag on the wall.

## 2 · De Festival-Flamingo — signature colour: **hot flamingo pink**

100% festival, always first on the dancefloor, the "ik kom eraan!"-texter who's
still on the couch. Spontaneous, loud, joyful.

- **Character:** big grin, mid-motion energy, phone in hand (texting "kom
  eraan!"), glitter on the cheeks, wristbands stacked up the arm. Long limbs
  (flamingo).
- **Outfit:** **flamingo-pink** windbreaker/cropped festival top, bold patterns,
  festival lanyard.
- **Pose:** dynamic, caught mid-dance / mid-stride, hair and jacket in motion,
  light speed-lines.
- **Setting tint:** neon-pink graffiti, festival flags and bunting.

## 3 · De Salmari-Soldaat — signature colour: **electric grape / purple**

Marches into the city, straight to the dancefloor, powers through on shots and
"nog even één drankje" — then the vanishing trick. Mischievous, spontaneous.

- **Character:** sly smirk, one eyebrow up, carrying a small tray of black
  salmiakki shots; caught **mid-vanish** (a faint motion after-image / half-fading
  edge).
- **Outfit:** dark **purple/grape** streetwear bomber, hood down, chunky boots,
  edgy layered look.
- **Pose:** turning to leave while glancing back, drink raised.
- **Setting tint:** purple night-alley graffiti, distant city lights.

## 4 · De Lange Nachtbraker — signature colour: **electric indigo / night**

The night owl. Comes alive when everyone else yawns, nurses "nog één drankje"
till deep in the night, watches from the sideline. The tallest of the set.

- **Character:** heavy-lidded content smile, relaxed, leaning against the wall
  with a single drink; a bit dreamy. Emphasise the **height** (very long legs).
- **Outfit:** oversized **indigo** hoodie, beanie, joggers, comfy high-tops.
- **Pose:** leaning, one foot against the wall, drink in hand, chill.
- **Setting tint:** deep indigo night graffiti, warm string lights overhead.

## 5 · De Verantwoordelijke Kompaan — signature colour: **acid / park green**

The loyal one. Comes for the group, leaves on time for the last train, sticks
with the vaste kliek, the quiet ghost in the app. Warm and reliable.

- **Character:** warm reliable smile, glancing at a watch/phone (last train),
  tote over the shoulder; grounded and steady.
- **Outfit:** cozy **green** cardigan or varsity jacket over a tee, comfy scarf
  (small giraffe-print detail), practical shoes.
- **Pose:** mid-wave goodbye to the group, half-turned, friendly.
- **Setting tint:** green leafy alley graffiti, soft late-afternoon daylight.

## 6 · De Bedtijd-Baron — signature colour: **deep wine red**

Brings the vibe, pours the wine, makes the kroegborrel — then rules over his own
bedtime and is gone by midnight; a secret morning person. Dapper and warm.

- **Character:** relaxed regal air with a sleepy twinkle, glass of red wine in
  hand, a slightly crown-like beanie ("baron"), cozy scarf.
- **Outfit:** dapper-casual — **wine-red** overshirt/cardigan, clean trousers,
  loafers; a small giraffe-print pocket square.
- **Pose:** toasting the viewer while half-turned toward the exit.
- **Setting tint:** wine-red pub-alley graffiti, warm evening glow, string lights.

## Set-cohesion checklist (so the six look like one poster series)

- Same art style, line weight, shading and grain on all six.
- Same framing: full-body, ¾ pose, 4:5 portrait, subject centered with headroom.
- Same lighting direction and warmth; only the **signature colour** and the
  graffiti-wall tint change per type.
- All tall (giraffe motif) with one small giraffe-print accent each.
- Keep a clean margin / plain background edge for cut-out placement on the site.
- Generate as a batch with a fixed style reference / seed for consistency.
